# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Three kinds of people run Kitchen IN, a food and grocery delivery marketplace
operating in Egypt (and ready for Saudi Arabia), in Arabic and English:

- **Operators (admin console, desktop web).** Platform staff with scoped roles
  — operations, finance, catalogue, support. They sit at a monitor for hours:
  watching live orders, approving stores and drivers, settling money,
  answering support, running promotions. Their job is to notice what needs
  them and act on it quickly without making a costly mistake with money.
- **Store owners and staff (store console, desktop web and phone).** Running a
  kitchen or shop: accepting and preparing orders, keeping the menu and
  opening hours right, checking what they are owed. Often interrupted, often
  on a busy counter.
- **Customers and drivers (phone apps).** Out of scope for the consoles, but
  they share models, copy and brand.

## Product Purpose

Connect customers with local stores and drivers, and give the people running
the platform and the stores one place to run the business. Success is an
operator who can tell at a glance what needs them, settle a store or a driver
without a spreadsheet on the side, and never be surprised by the money; and a
store that trusts the numbers it is shown.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. A console that is quiet until something needs
attention, puts numbers first, and speaks plainly in both languages. The
brand (aubergine and pistachio) is present, not loud: colour marks state and
the one thing that matters on a screen, not decoration.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS templates: identical card grids, gradient hero numbers, the
  purple/blue startup look, an icon-heading-text card for every idea.
- Old ERP / Excel-like back offices: grey tables everywhere, tiny text, no
  hierarchy, every control the same weight.
- A consumer phone app stretched onto a monitor: centred narrow columns, huge
  buttons, floating action buttons, stacked cards where a table belongs.

## Design Principles

1. **Say what needs you.** Every page opens with what it is for and surfaces
   what is waiting; calm when nothing is.
2. **Money is exact and explained.** Amounts are aligned, in the platform
   currency, and every total says what it is made of and for which period.
   Destructive or money-moving actions confirm in plain words.
3. **Density with hierarchy.** Desktop pages use the width — tables, two
   columns, side panels — but one thing leads each screen.
4. **Same parts everywhere.** Admin and store consoles are built from the
   same console kit, so a pattern learned once works on every page.
5. **Plain, bilingual words.** Sentence case, the operator's vocabulary,
   Arabic that reads as Egyptian Arabic, errors that say how to fix them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 body text contrast (3:1 for large text), visible keyboard
focus, full keyboard use on desktop web, complete right-to-left layout for
Arabic, colour never the only signal of state, reduced motion respected.
